---
order: 11
title: Publish original data
when: The category lacks credible numbers, and the client has product data, survey access or rare expertise.
effort: L
firstSignal: 2–6 months
steps:
  - Pick a question buyers and journalists actually ask
  - Collect the data with a defensible method, and publish the methodology
  - Put the key numbers in plain text, easy to quote
  - Pitch the findings to category publications and communities
moves:
  - referring-domains
  - citation-share
  - third-party-presence
watchOut: Weak methodology gets picked apart in public. Have the method reviewed before launch.
