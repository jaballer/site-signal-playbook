---
order: 11
title: Put real expertise on the page
when: Priority pages are unsigned, undated or generic, and read as though anyone could have written them.
effort: M
firstSignal: months
owner: content
timing: Two to four months. It works by making every other content play land harder, so judge it on the pages it touches, not on its own.
steps:
  - Give every priority page a named author with a real bio, credentials and a link to their profiles
  - Add one thing to each page that only this company could write, such as a customer number, a support pattern, a benchmark from their own data or a photograph of the actual product
  - Cite sources for every claim, and say where a figure came from and when
  - Show a visible, honest last-reviewed date, and put the page on a review cycle
  - Have a subject-matter expert review the page and record that they did
moves:
  - content-quality
  - entity-consistency
  - citation-share
dependsOn:
  - entity-foundation
related:
  - original-data
watchOut: A byline on a page the named person didn't write or review is worse than no byline, and clients get caught. Either get the expert's time or don't claim it.
---
