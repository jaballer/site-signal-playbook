---
order: 8
title: Own the category's definitions
when: Buyers and AI engines ask what something is, and the answers come from competitors or from generic publishers.
effort: M
firstSignal: weeks
owner: content
timing: Weeks for AI answers and featured snippets, two to four months for rankings.
steps:
  - Pull the definitional prompts and "what is" queries out of the prompt and keyword sets
  - Write one page per term, opening with a direct two-sentence definition before any context
  - Put the facts a machine can lift in plain text, a short definition, a list of the parts, a table where something is compared, and the one thing people get wrong
  - Link each definition to the product or solution page it supports, and to the cluster it belongs to
  - Re-run the matching prompts monthly and rewrite the pages the engines still summarize away
moves:
  - extractability
  - citation-share
  - topic-coverage
  - serp-features
related:
  - answer-first-pages
  - topic-cluster
watchOut: These pages attract researchers, not buyers, so judge them on citations and assisted pipeline rather than on their own conversion rate. A client who reads them as poor-converting traffic will want to cut them.
