---
order: 14
title: Run the review program
when: The category's review platforms are cited in AI answers and rank for comparison terms, and the client's profiles are thin, stale or missing.
effort: M
firstSignal: months
owner: off-site
timing: Two to four months to build enough recent reviews to move a category ranking, then ongoing to hold it.
steps:
  - Pick the two or three platforms that actually get cited and rank for the category, from the cited-sources list, rather than every platform that sells a listing
  - Complete each profile, with current pricing, features, screenshots, integrations and the right categories
  - Ask happy customers for reviews on a schedule the client's team can sustain, through customer success rather than marketing
  - Keep review recency up, since most platforms weight recent reviews and so do the engines that read them
  - Answer every negative review, and feed the pattern back to the client's product team
  - Check the comparison pages these platforms publish about the client, and request corrections where the facts are wrong
moves:
  - third-party-presence
  - cited-sources
  - branded-demand
related:
  - win-cited-sources
  - correct-ai-answers
watchOut: Review gating, incentives that aren't disclosed and reviews written by staff break platform rules and get profiles delisted. Volume also decays, so a burst of reviews followed by a year of silence reads worse than a steady trickle.
---
