---
term: Schema
expansion: schema.org structured data
aliases:
  - Structured data
  - JSON-LD
definition: Machine-readable labels added to a page, usually as JSON-LD, that state what it is and what it says, such as an organization, a product, an article or its author.
signals:
  - structured-data
  - entity-consistency
questions:
  - crawler-readiness
related:
  - entity
  - knowledge-panel
---

It doesn't make a page rank, and it isn't a substitute for saying things clearly in text. What it does is remove ambiguity about facts the engine would otherwise have to infer, which matters most for organization, author and product details.
