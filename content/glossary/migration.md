definition: Any change to a site's URLs, platform, templates or domain. From a search point of view, a redesign that changes URLs is a migration, whatever else it's called internally.

The risk isn't the new design, it's everything the old site had that nobody wrote down: the URLs, the copy, the internal links, the schema and the tracking. Benchmark before launch, because after launch there's nothing to compare against.
