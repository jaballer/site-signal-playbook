---
term: Canonical
expansion: canonical tag
definition: A tag telling search engines which URL is the preferred version of a page when several show similar content. Engines treat it as a strong hint, not an instruction.
signals:
  - index-coverage
  - crawlability
questions:
  - crawler-readiness
related:
  - noindex
  - redirect-map
  - cannibalization
---

Wrong canonicals are one of the most common causes of a section quietly dropping out of the index after a release, because the page looks fine to everyone except the crawler.
