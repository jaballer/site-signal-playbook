---
term: Attribution ceiling
definition: The limit on how much of search's contribution any analytics setup can actually show. Long B2B journeys cross devices and months, and an AI answer that influences a buyer without a click leaves no record at all.
signals:

---

Naming it on day one builds more credibility than promising precision later. The honest position is that reported pipeline is a floor, not a total, and that the gap is a reason to report citations and impressions beside clicks.
