---
term: Index coverage
definition: How many of a site's pages search engines have actually indexed, and the reasons the rest were excluded. Search Console reports it per site, with a reason for each exclusion.
signals:
  - index-coverage
questions:
  - crawler-readiness
related:
  - canonical
  - noindex
  - crawl-budget
---

A page that isn't indexed can't rank, can't be cited and can't be fixed by better content. It's the first thing to check when a section loses traffic, and the exclusion reason usually names the investigation.
