---
term: llms.txt
definition: A proposed file at the root of a site listing the pages worth reading, in plain Markdown, to help AI systems find the important content. It's a convention, not a standard, and support varies.
signals:
  - ai-crawler-access
questions:
  - crawler-readiness
related:
  - robots-txt
  - ai-crawler
---

Cheap to publish and keep current, so it's worth doing. It isn't a fix for a site AI crawlers can't reach or can't read, and anyone selling it as one is selling the wrong thing.
