---
term: Crawl budget
definition: How much crawling an engine is willing to do on a site, set by how fast the site responds and how much the engine thinks is worth fetching. It matters on large sites and rarely on small ones.
signals:
  - crawlability
  - cwv
questions:
  - crawler-readiness
related:
  - index-coverage
  - robots-txt
---

It gets blamed for indexing problems it didn't cause. On a site of a few hundred pages, pages aren't missing because the engine ran out of budget; they're missing for a reason Search Console will name.
