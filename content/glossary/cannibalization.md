---
term: Cannibalization
definition: Two or more pages on the same site competing for the same query, so neither accumulates the links, engagement and relevance it would have had alone.
signals:
  - cannibalization
questions:
  - content-decisions
related:
  - canonical
  - pillar-page
---

The usual fix is consolidation, not more content. Merge the pages into the strongest one, redirect the rest, and give each remaining page a distinct job.
