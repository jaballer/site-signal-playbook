---
term: Pillar page
aliases:
  - Topic cluster
  - Hub and spoke
definition: The main page for a topic, giving the broad answer and linking out to supporting pages that each cover one part of it in depth. The pillar and its supporting pages together are the cluster.
signals:
  - topic-coverage
  - internal-links
questions:
  - content-decisions
related:
  - topical-authority
  - cannibalization
---

The structure matters less than the discipline behind it: one page per distinct job, each linked to the others, so the site has an obvious best answer for every part of the topic instead of five overlapping ones.
