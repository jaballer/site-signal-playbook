---
term: E-E-A-T
expansion: experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trustworthiness
definition: The qualities Google's human quality raters are asked to judge in a page and the site behind it. It isn't a score in the ranking system, but the systems are built and tested against it.
signals:
  - content-quality
  - third-party-presence

Treat it as a description of what good looks like, not a checklist to game. In practice it means a named author who genuinely knows the subject, something on the page only this company could have written, sources for the claims, and a reputation that exists off the site.
