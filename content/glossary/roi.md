---
term: ROI
expansion: return on investment
definition: What a program returns compared with what it costs, usually calculated as (return − cost) ÷ cost and shown as a percentage.
signals:
  - opportunity-value
  - search-pipeline
questions:

For search, the return is the pipeline and revenue it produces. The playbook sizes it up front with an opportunity model, then reports search-sourced pipeline against program cost.
