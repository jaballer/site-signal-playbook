---
term: Citation share
aliases:
  - Mention share
definition: Across a fixed set of prompts, how often an AI engine cites a brand's site, or names the brand, compared with competitors. Citation means the source is linked; mention means the brand is named in the text.
signals:
  - citation-share
  - cited-sources
questions:
  - ai-answers
  - competitors
related:
  - prompt-set
  - geo
  - sov
---

It's the AI-answer equivalent of share of voice, and the measure that keeps working when clicks stop. Because answers vary between runs, it only means something across a whole prompt set over months, never from one screenshot.
