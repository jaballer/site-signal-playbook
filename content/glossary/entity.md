---
term: Entity
definition: A thing a search or AI engine recognises as a distinct, real-world subject, such as a company, a product or a person, rather than just a string of words. Engines connect entities to the facts they hold about them.

If an engine isn't sure the company is a single, well-defined thing, it hedges or gets the facts wrong. Consistent facts across the site, schema and third-party profiles are what resolve that, and it's why entity work usually comes before AI visibility work.
