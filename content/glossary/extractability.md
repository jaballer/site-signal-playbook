---
term: Extractability
aliases:
  - Answer-first structure
definition: How easily an engine can lift a clear, self-contained answer from a page. A page is extractable when it answers the question in its opening lines, uses question-style headings, and keeps facts in text rather than in images, tabs or scripts.
signals:
  - extractability
  - citation-share
questions:
  - content-decisions
related:
  - citation-share
  - geo
---

It's the on-page half of AI visibility. A page can be accurate, well-linked and crawlable and still never be quoted, because the answer is buried three paragraphs into a build-up nobody asked for.
