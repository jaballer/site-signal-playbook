---
term: Core Web Vitals
expansion: LCP, INP and CLS
definition: Google's three field measures of page experience, covering how quickly the main content appears, how quickly the page responds to an interaction, and how much the layout shifts while loading.
signals:
  - cwv
questions:
  - crawler-readiness
related:
  - crawl-budget
---

Field data from real visits is the measure that counts, and it's a rolling window, so a fix takes about a month to show in the reported number. Treat speed as a conversion and crawl-efficiency argument rather than a ranking one.
