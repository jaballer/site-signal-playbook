term: Attribution
aliases:
  - First-touch
  - Last-click
  - Original source
definition: The rule deciding which channel gets credit for a lead or a deal. First-touch credits the first visit, last-click the last one before the conversion. The CRM's original-source field is usually the first-touch record.

There's no correct rule, only a rule everyone agreed to in advance. Changing it mid-program looks like moving the goalposts even when it's the right call, so it goes in writing before the first report.
