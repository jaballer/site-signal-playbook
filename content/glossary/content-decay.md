---
term: Content decay
definition: The steady loss of traffic from a page that once performed, as the information ages, competitors publish better versions, or the query's intent shifts.
signals:

questions:

It's the cheapest win in most content programs, because the page already has links, history and a position to defend. Refreshing a decaying page usually beats writing a new one.
