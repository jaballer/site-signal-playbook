definition: In search, the share of available clicks a brand captures across a set of keywords, compared with competitors. In AI search, the equivalent is how often a brand is mentioned or cited across a set of prompts.

Traffic alone can't show whether you're winning the market; share of voice can, especially when it's tracked by topic against named competitors.
