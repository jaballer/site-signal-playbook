---
term: SERP
expansion: search engine results page
aliases:
  - Results page
definition: The page a search engine shows for a query. Alongside the standard links it can include ads, an AI Overview, featured snippets, People Also Ask boxes, videos and forum results.
signals:
  - serp-features
  - ctr-by-position
related:
  - ctr
  - geo
---

A page can keep its ranking and still lose clicks when new features push it further down, so reports look at what's on the SERP, not just the position.
