question: Are we more visible than competitors where it matters?
definition: Estimated share of available clicks across a fixed keyword set, grouped by topic and compared with named competitors.
source: Rank tracker (Ahrefs, Semrush or similar)
capture: Build the keyword set from priority topics, tag each keyword by topic and buyer stage, and add three to five named competitors. Freeze the set; changes are dated revisions.
read: Compare by topic, not overall
---

Winning the glossary while losing 'pricing' and 'alternatives' looks like growth in the overall number. It isn't. Visibility scores also differ by vendor, so never compare numbers across tools.
