---
title: Migration and redesign risk
layer: foundations
phases:
  - audit
  - execute
question: Will a redesign or URL change keep its search equity?
definition: Redirect coverage from old to new URLs, preserved titles, content and internal links, and clicks before versus after.
source: Pre-launch crawl, redirect map, Search Console
capture: Benchmark before launch, crawl staging, check redirects on launch day, and monitor daily for four weeks.
read: Clicks back to the pre-launch level within about two months
---

The biggest risk is being brought in after launch. Ask about planned redesigns at kickoff.
