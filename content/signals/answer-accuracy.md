---
title: Accuracy of AI answers about the brand
layer: ai-visibility
phases:
  - audit
  - report
question: Are AI engines getting our facts right?
definition: For brand prompts (pricing, features, integrations, security, who it's for), whether each stated fact is correct, outdated or wrong.
source: Prompt runs
capture: Score each fact, and record the source the engine cites for it.
read: Any wrong pricing or capability claim is a priority fix
---

Wrong answers usually trace to a third-party page or an old page on the client's own site. Fix the source, then state the fact plainly on a page the client controls.
