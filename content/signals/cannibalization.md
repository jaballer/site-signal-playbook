---
title: Keyword cannibalization
layer: content-authority
phases:
  - audit
  - execute
question: Are our own pages competing with each other?
definition: Queries where two or more of the site's URLs rank and trade positions.
source: Search Console query-to-page data, rank tracker
capture: Flag priority queries with more than one ranking URL over the period.
read: Consolidate, redirect or re-target; internal links alone rarely fix it
---

Blog sprawl on one topic is the usual cause. Consolidation often lifts the surviving page faster than publishing something new.
