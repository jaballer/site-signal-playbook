title: Content decay
layer: content-authority
phases:
  - execute
  - report
question: Which pages are losing clicks they used to earn?
definition: Pages whose clicks declined against the same period last year.
source: Search Console Pages report
capture: Compare the last three months with the same months last year for each page, and rank by clicks lost.
read: Refresh pages that still match intent; merge or retire the rest
---

A refresh program against decaying pages is usually the fastest measurable win on a mature site.
