---
order: 10
question: What is search worth to us, and what should we invest in next?
askedBy: Leadership at planning time, or in a quarterly review.
timebox: Half a day
expect: The realistic upside as a range, three priorities with expected impact and effort, and what the client needs to provide.
steps:
  - Run the [trust check](question:trust-the-numbers). Anything it flags becomes priority zero.
  - "For each priority topic, estimate the upside: search demand × expected click-through at the target position × conversion rate × lead value. Build low, expected and high cases."
  - List candidate plays from the audit and recent reports, and score each on revenue at stake, confidence and effort (see [Plays](page:plays)).
  - Pick three. Blockers to crawling, indexing or measurement go first regardless of score.
  - Write the expected result and leading indicator for each, and what the client needs to supply.
read:
  - Present ranges and assumptions before the number. A single forecast gets remembered as a promise.
  - Include AI visibility in the upside, but value it on the leads it can plausibly drive, not on the hype.
  - "'We can't measure this yet' is a legitimate finding. Put a cost on closing the gap."
beforeYouStart: Bring the change log and last quarter's expected-versus-actual results. Leaders fund teams whose past estimates held up.
script: Search could add [low]–[high] leads a quarter within [timeframe], worth [value range] in pipeline. Our top priorities are [play] ([impact]), [play] and [play]. We need [dependency] from your team to get there.
