title: AI visibility dropped
inTheField: A stakeholder forwards a screenshot of an assistant recommending three competitors and not the client. The prompt panel shows citation share on category prompts slipping over two months, concentrated in one engine. In the same window, a competitor published a comparison page, and a widely cited review site refreshed its category list without the client.
steps:
  - title: Check it against the prompt set, not the screenshot
    detail: AI answers vary from run to run, so one answer proves nothing. Confirm the change across the full set and at least two runs.
  - title: Split by engine and by prompt type
    detail: A drop in one engine points to that engine's sources. A drop across engines on comparison prompts points to the category's sources changing.
  - title: Compare the cited sources before and after
    detail: New domains being cited is the most common cause. The list tells you where to act.
  - title: Check access and accuracy
    detail: Confirm AI crawlers still reach priority pages (robots.txt, CDN rules, logs), and that nothing important moved behind JavaScript, a login or a redirect.
  - title: Check what competitors shipped
    detail: New comparison pages, studies, review campaigns or list placements in the window.
  - title: Respond at the source
    detail: Win back the cited sources, publish the page the answers are missing, and correct stale facts. Re-run the affected prompts monthly.
