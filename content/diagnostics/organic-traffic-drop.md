title: Organic traffic dropped
inTheField: Leadership sees organic sessions down year over year and asks whether SEO stopped working. Split apart, it's several smaller stories. Branded demand softened with the market. A group of informational queries gained AI Overviews and lost clicks at stable rankings. One template lost indexing after a release. And part of the "drop" is a tracking change in GA4.
steps:
  - title: Confirm it's real, and where it's measured
    detail: Compare Search Console clicks with GA4 organic sessions for the same period. If Search Console is flat and GA4 fell, the cause is tracking or consent, not search.
  - title: Split branded from non-branded
    detail: A branded decline is a brand and demand question. Only a non-branded decline belongs to the search program.
  - title: Find the pages and queries that lost clicks
    detail: In Search Console, compare periods on the Pages and Queries tabs and sort by clicks lost. Declines are almost always concentrated.
  - title: Separate ranking loss from click loss
    detail: For the losing queries, did position fall, or did CTR fall at the same position? Stable position with falling CTR points to AI Overviews or new results-page features. Look at the results page.
  - title: Check the timeline
    detail: Line the drop up against Google's Search Status Dashboard, site releases, migrations, and robots or CDN changes.
  - title: Check the losing pages for technical regressions
    detail: Indexing status, canonicals, noindex tags, rendering and internal links on the pages that fell.
  - title: Report the components, then the plan
    detail: Present the drop as its parts, each with an owner and a response, rather than one number and one explanation.
