title: Leads fell, traffic didn't
inTheField: "Leadership arrives with one total: organic leads down while traffic is flat. Split by form and landing page, a newly added popup is growing while two embedded forms fall away. Meanwhile, conversion-event volume spiked on exactly the days the tag container was published. Neither fact is visible in the total, and both change what to fix."
steps:
  - title: Validate the baseline before the decline
    detail: Overlay daily conversion volume on the change log. Spikes within a few days of a tag publish or release are testing or double-firing, not demand, and they inflate the baseline.
  - title: Check for duplicate firing
    detail: Run Tag Assistant on conversion and thank-you pages. Tags firing twice mean every conversion number is wrong.
  - title: Break the total down by form and landing page
    detail: "A uniform decline points to something sitewide: tracking, consent, speed. A concentrated one points to specific pages."
  - title: Check the traffic mix
    detail: "Flat sessions can hide a shift: more informational traffic from new content converts at a lower rate than product-page traffic. Compare conversion rate by buyer stage."
  - title: Test whether leads just moved
    detail: If the number of people submitting any form is flat, a new form or popup is taking submissions from the old ones.
  - title: Only then check speed, form errors and CRM sync
    detail: All real causes, and all rarely the first one.
