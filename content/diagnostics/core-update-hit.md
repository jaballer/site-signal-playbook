title: A Google update landed on us
inTheField: A core update rolls out over two weeks. Traffic falls through it, the client has read three contradictory threads about what the update targeted, and wants to know what to change on Monday. The honest answer is that nothing should change on Monday, and that saying so is the job.
steps:
  - title: Confirm the dates against the Search Status Dashboard
    detail: Get the exact start and end of the rollout. A decline that began before it started, or continued long after it ended, is not the update, and treating it as one buries the real cause.
  - title: Wait for the rollout to finish before drawing conclusions
    detail: Rankings move in both directions during a rollout. Reading day four of a fourteen-day update produces a diagnosis that is wrong and a set of changes that have to be undone.
  - title: Check whether it's a site-wide or a section-level move
    detail: Compare before and after by template, by topic cluster and by buyer stage. Core updates usually revalue a kind of content rather than a whole domain, and the pattern says which kind.
  - title: Look at who took the positions
    detail: For the queries that fell, look at what now ranks. A competitor with deeper coverage, a review site, a forum thread or an AI Overview each imply a different response, and only one of them is about the client's own pages.
  - title: Read the affected pages against the quality questions
    detail: Take the worst-hit pages and ask whether they carry first-hand experience, a named author who knows the subject, original information, and a reason for a human to prefer them. That's the assessment, not a checklist of on-page factors.
  - title: Hold the line on the roadmap
    detail: Resist reshipping the roadmap around the update. Recovery usually comes with a later update, and the work that earns it is the same work that was already ranked by revenue at stake.
  - title: Tell the client what you're doing and when you'll know
    detail: Send a short note during the rollout saying what you've observed, what you're waiting on and the date you'll come back with a read. Silence during an update is what loses accounts.
