title: Clicks fell but rankings held
inTheField: A client sends the Search Console graph with impressions flat or rising and clicks falling, and asks what went wrong with the rankings. Nothing did. A set of informational queries picked up AI Overviews over six weeks, and those queries now resolve on the results page. The pages still rank where they did. The commercial queries in the same account are untouched.
steps:
  - title: Confirm position held while CTR fell
    detail: In Search Console, compare periods on the Queries tab with clicks, impressions, CTR and position all shown. Falling CTR at a stable position is the pattern. Falling position is a different problem, and belongs in the traffic-drop flow.
  - title: Split the queries by whether an AI Overview appears
    detail: Take the queries that lost the most clicks and check the live results page for each, or use a rank tracker's results-page feature data. Group them into "AI Overview present" and "not present", and compare CTR at similar positions across the two groups. That comparison is the measurement, and it belongs in the report.
  - title: Check whether the brand is cited in those answers
    detail: Being cited in an AI Overview is a different outcome from being absent from one. Cited and losing clicks is a measurement problem. Absent and losing clicks is a visibility problem, and a much more urgent one.
  - title: Check the buyer stage of the affected queries
    detail: Informational queries losing clicks to AI answers costs less than it looks, as long as the commercial queries hold. Commercial queries losing clicks is the serious case. Report the two separately.
  - title: Look for the other features too
    detail: AI Overviews get the blame, but expanded People Also Ask, video packs, forum results and a new paid block above the fold all take clicks the same way.
  - title: Reset the measure, then respond
    detail: Move the report for these queries onto impressions, citation share and assisted pipeline, and say why. Then make the pages easier to cite, and go after the sources the answers draw on.
