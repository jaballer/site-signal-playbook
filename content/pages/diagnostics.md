lede: Situations that recur on every account. Each is a fixed order of checks, because the cheapest checks most often end the investigation, and because the expensive ones produce confident wrong answers when run first.
blocks:
  - type: strip
    heading: How to run one
    intro: The same four rules apply to every flow below, and skipping them is how an investigation ends in a confident wrong answer.
    items:
      - title: Check the measurement before the cause
        text: A number can move because something broke in the tracking. That check is nearly free and it ends a surprising share of investigations.
      - title: Split the total before explaining it
        text: One number is usually several stories. [Branded and non-branded](term:branded-search), by template, by [buyer stage](term:buyer-stage), by engine. Explain the parts, not the total.
      - title: Overlay the change log
        text: Most unexplained movements line up with a release, a tag publish, a robots change or a Google update. Look there before theorising.
      - title: End in a play, not a theory
        text: Every flow finishes by naming what to run. A diagnosis nobody acts on costs the same as no diagnosis and looks worse.
    footnote: "Send the client a short note while an investigation is open, saying what you've seen and when you'll come back. **Silence during a drop is what loses accounts.**"
