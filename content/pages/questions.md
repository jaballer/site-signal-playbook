heading: The questions leadership asks, and how to answer them
lede: Step-by-step procedures for what a CMO, CEO or CFO expects a search program to tell them. Each covers who asks, what they expect to see, where to find it, how to read it, and a sentence to adapt for the report. On a new client, start with [Can we trust these numbers?](question:trust-the-numbers)
blocks:
  - type: strip
    label: Every answer runs the same loop
    items:
      - title: Trust
        text: Is the number real? Check the tracking before reading it.
      - title: Split
        text: Never report a total alone. Branded from non-branded, by topic, by page, by engine.
      - title: Compare
        text: Against last year, the baseline, and named competitors.
      - title: Explain
        text: Line the change up against what shipped, Google updates and results-page changes.
      - title: Act
        text: One change, an expected result, and a re-check next period.

    collection: questions
