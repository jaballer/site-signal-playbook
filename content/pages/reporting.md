heading: What leadership sees, and how often
lede: Reports lead with the answer, cover the whole chain from visibility to pipeline, and explain every movement with a cause. Traffic alone under-reports wins and hides losses once AI answers take clicks, so visibility and citations are reported beside it.
blocks:
  - type: panels
    heading: Cadence
    items:
      - title: Weekly, internal
        text: Leading indicators on shipped work, indexing of changed pages, crawl and tracking alerts, Google updates. Goes to the client only when something needs a decision.
      - title: Monthly, client
        text: The report below, sent with a 20-minute walkthrough. Decisions go in the decisions log.
      - title: Quarterly, leadership
        text: Results against targets and the opportunity model, competitive position, the revised roadmap, and a scope proposal.
  - type: table
    heading: The monthly report
    columns:
      - Section
      - Contents
      - Question it answers
    rows:
      - - 1 · Summary
        - "Three sentences: what moved, why, what's next. One decision needed from the client."
        - What do I need to know?
      - - 2 · Scorecard
        - The seven layers against baseline and target ranges, marked on track, at risk or off track
        - Are we on track?
      - - 3 · Search visibility
        - Non-branded clicks and impressions, share of voice by topic, priority rankings, CTR changes where AI Overviews appeared
        - Are we winning search?
      - - 4 · AI visibility
        - Mention and citation share against competitors, answer accuracy, changes in cited sources
        - Are we winning AI answers?
      - - 5 · Traffic & pipeline
        - Organic and AI sessions, leads by landing page, search-sourced pipeline against target
        - Is it paying off?
      - - 6 · Work shipped
        - What shipped, expected impact against early signal, what's next
        - What did we get for the fee?
      - - 7 · Integrity notes
        - Tracking caveats and data changes for the period
        - Can I trust this?
  - type: markdown
    heading: Reporting when clicks fall and visibility rises
    text: "AI Overviews and assistants answer questions before a click. Report impressions, citation share and branded demand as leading indicators next to clicks, and set that expectation at kickoff, not in the month traffic dips. Measure the effect directly: group priority keywords by whether an AI Overview appears, and compare CTR at similar positions."
  - type: panels
    heading: The attribution ceiling
    intro: "Last-click analytics undercounts search's role in long B2B buying journeys, and AI answers that influence a buyer without a click don't appear at all. Say this in the first review, not the fourth. Two honest options:"
    items:
      - eyebrow: Option A
        title: GA4 plus CRM original source
        text: Leads by landing page from GA4, pipeline by original source from the CRM. Directional, cheap, and enough for most programs.
      - eyebrow: Option B
        title: Warehouse join
        text: GA4's BigQuery export joined to CRM records for journey-level attribution. Solid, and the only way to show multi-touch influence. Costs setup and analyst time.
  - type: markdown
    text: Agree the attribution rule in writing before month one. Changing it mid-program looks like moving the goalposts, even when it's the right call.
